These short demonstrations use the creators’ public YouTube embeds. The videos are not downloaded or rehosted. Creator branding and original links remain visible.

Each selected source was reviewed for a real person, seated execution, a clear camera angle and an identifiable movement. Playback is muted with volume zero; the original sources may contain narration. Written instructions are localized separately.

The listed start/end times are the displayed excerpt, not the length of the original video. The player pauses at the end, supports replay and 0.75× speed, and is destroyed when leaving the video section.
