# Adult Coloring — beginner technique course

## Selection and relation to the supplied books

The rejected flower-process clips are no longer in any of the fifteen playlists.
The replacement is five small, repeatable exercises. Each action is filmed with
real hands; there are no animated demonstrations. Descriptions name the actual
materials, the steps describe the visible strokes, and related-book links point
to the appropriate supplied PDFs. These are complementary technique exercises
on separate sample shapes, not footage of the supplied coloring pages.

The supplied PDFs suggest markers for solid color, gel for details, color-family
palettes, repeated decorative colors and several greens for foliage. Their actual
line art includes cozy rooms/furniture/textiles (Book 1), garden doors and plants
(Book 2) and repeated mandala shapes (Book 3). The five exercises cover controlled
fill, wood details, three green values, layered stripes and dots/gel finishing.
No claim is made that the PDFs contain full technical lessons or that every
possible coloring technique has been covered.

## Primary footage and edits

Creator: Niki’s Cozy Corner.

1. **Even fill** — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S1wISGj2U6Q
   Source 10.8–21.3, slowed to 0.5x: 21 seconds. Blank sample to filled base.
   Three labeled stages, continuous playback because wet edges should not dry.
2. **Wood grain** — same source. 28.5–45.5, 48–68, 71.3–87.2, at 1x:
   52.9 seconds. Prepared dry ochre base → light veins → darker knots → warm
   additional veins. Changes of marker/cap presentations are omitted. The dry
   starting base is explicitly the prerequisite from lesson 1.
3. **Three greens** — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qzLlQavNUw8
   20.5–30.5, 31.3–66, 67–106, at 1.42x: about 58.94 seconds.
   Retains the actual tip-to-tip loading demonstrations and all three color
   applications. Continuous playback with changing stage instructions preserves
   the wet blending workflow. Learners are told to observe first, then repeat.
4. **Simple plaid** — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wZNPJcz7qBY
   5.5–58.5, at 1x: 53 seconds. Base → vertical stripes → horizontal stripes,
   complete simple plaid. The later optional white-gel embellishment is excluded;
   the lesson only claims to teach the simple plaid shown in this selection.

Creator: Pillie and Pop.

5. **Dots and white gel** — https://www.pillieandpop.com/post/how-to-color-glitter-wrapping-paper
   Original video: https://video.wixstatic.com/video/857509_a504770f55844b99838872d9655501f4/720p/mp4/file.mp4
   4.2–36, slowed to 0.6x: 53 seconds. Light base → midtone dots → darker dots →
   white gel stars/dots. Only this decorative texture is taught; the later ribbon
   demonstration is excluded. Instructions retain the author's 30–60 second drying
   waits between layers and before gel. Those waits are practice time, not film time.

## Player and localization

Five films in every locale: de, fr, it, nl, sv, da, es, fi, el, hu, no, pl, pt,
ro and cs. Native-language objectives/materials, visible instructions, preparation
and practice prompts. Still posters show actual finished sample results. Learners
can repeat a stage or use 0.75x playback. All audio streams are removed in the MP4
files. No third-party player, platform identifier or platform overlay is displayed.
Creator credit remains below the film. No creator watermark is removed.
